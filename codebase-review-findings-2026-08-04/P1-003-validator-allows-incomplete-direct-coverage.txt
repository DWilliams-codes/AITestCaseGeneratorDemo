ID: P1-003
Priority: P1 High
Category: Generation semantics / traceability
Status: Confirmed validator omission

Defect
The semantic validator ensures a direct case has at least one allowed criterion key, but never proves
that every supplied acceptance criterion has at least one DIRECT case. A response covering AC-1 and
omitting AC-2 passes.

Evidence
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/generation/validation/GenerationResultValidator.java:44-51
  computes allowedKeys but does not accumulate covered keys.
- same file:86-94 validates only each case's local mapping.
- docs/product-specs/mvp-1-test-generation.md:70 requires direct coverage for every criterion.
- evals/RUBRIC.md:20 makes every supplied criterion with direct evidence a core score.

Impact
Incomplete provider output can be marked COMPLETED, persisted, reviewed, and exported despite failing
the MVP's defining traceability requirement.

Remediation
Accumulate normalized keys from ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA/DIRECT cases, reject duplicates as appropriate,
and require equality with the supplied criterion-key set. Add unit fixtures for one omitted key,
supporting-only coverage, duplicate mappings, and complete multi-criterion coverage.
