ID: P1-009
Priority: P1 High
Category: Traceability metrics / review evidence
Status: Confirmed calculation defect

Defect
CoverageService treats any linked test case as covered and any approved linked case as approved
coverage, regardless of CoverageType. A SUPPORTING or PARTIAL case can therefore satisfy the same
metric as DIRECT acceptance-criterion evidence.

Evidence
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/traceability/application/TraceabilityService.java:91-109
  counts nonempty rows and APPROVED status but never inspects coverageType.
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/testcase/domain/CoverageType.java
  explicitly distinguishes DIRECT, PARTIAL, and SUPPORTING.
- evals/RUBRIC.md:20 requires direct evidence for every criterion.

Impact
Dashboards can show 100% covered and 100% approved when criteria have only exploratory/supporting
evidence. This gives reviewers and exports a materially false completion signal.

Remediation
Define and expose separate direct, partial, and supporting metrics. The primary acceptance coverage
and approved coverage percentages should require DIRECT links. Add tests covering approved supporting-
only, partial-only, mixed, and direct evidence.
