ID: P2-009
Priority: P2 Medium
Category: Audit data minimization
Status: Confirmed

Defect
The full user-supplied reopen reason (up to 4,000 characters) is copied into audit-event metadata.
AuditService accepts and serializes arbitrary maps without a key allowlist, sensitivity rule, or
metadata-size limit.

Evidence
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/testcase/application/TestCaseService.java:189-206
  passes the raw reason to Map.of.
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/testcase/dto/TestCaseDtos.java:61-62
  allows 4,000 characters.
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/audit/application/AuditService.java:24-52
  serializes arbitrary supplied metadata.
- docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:84 says audit metadata is intentionally small and non-sensitive.

Impact
Users can place customer data, secrets, or large free-form content into a long-lived audit channel,
increasing disclosure and retention risk and duplicating content outside its intended record.

Remediation
Create typed, allowlisted metadata DTOs per event. Store only a bounded reason classification/hash or
reference to the controlled revision/review record; keep the full reason in its domain field if needed.
Add tests rejecting oversize and disallowed metadata keys/values.
