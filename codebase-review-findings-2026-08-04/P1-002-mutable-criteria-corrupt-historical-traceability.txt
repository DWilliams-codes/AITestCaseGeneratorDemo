ID: P1-002
Priority: P1 High
Category: Evidence integrity / historical traceability
Status: Confirmed schema and service defect

Defect
Completed generation sets are described as immutable, but traceability links reference the mutable
current acceptance_criteria rows. Renaming or editing a criterion rewrites the meaning displayed for
old generation sets; deleting it cascades and permanently deletes historical links.

Evidence
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/requirement/application/RequirementService.java:177-221
  updates and deletes current criterion rows.
- backend/src/main/resources/db/migration/V2__stage_one_domain.sql:164
  traceability_links.acceptance_criterion_id uses ON DELETE CASCADE.
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/traceability/application/TraceabilityService.java:45-88
  builds historical matrices from current criterion rows and links.
- docs/PRODUCT.md:39-42 and docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:67-70
  promise immutable, readable historical generation sets.

Impact
Review and audit evidence no longer proves what the provider was asked to cover. A routine criterion
edit can silently change an old matrix, while deletion can erase evidence entirely.

Remediation
Persist an immutable criterion snapshot (stable key, description, ordering, source revision) per
generation set and link cases to that snapshot. Do not cascade current-source deletion into
historical evidence. Add tests that edit/delete current criteria and verify old matrices and exports
remain byte-for-byte semantically stable.
