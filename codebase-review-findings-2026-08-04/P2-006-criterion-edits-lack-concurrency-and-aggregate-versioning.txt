ID: P2-006
Priority: P2 Medium
Category: Optimistic concurrency / aggregate consistency
Status: Confirmed

Defect
Acceptance-criterion add/update/delete requests carry no expected version, and the entity has no
@Version field. These operations also do not touch the owning RequirementEntity or create a
requirement revision, so the requirement version/updatedAt can remain unchanged while its defining
criteria change.

Evidence
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/requirement/dto/RequirementDtos.java:64-76
  criterion request/response contain no version.
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/requirement/domain/AcceptanceCriterionEntity.java
  has no @Version.
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/requirement/application/RequirementService.java:147-221
  mutates criteria without assertVersion, requirement update, or saveRevision.
- docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:65 says optimistic versions prevent lost updates.

Impact
Concurrent criterion edits can silently overwrite each other. Cached requirement summaries and
revision/audit views do not expose a coherent aggregate revision for generation inputs.

Remediation
Choose an aggregate concurrency model: require the owning requirement version for every criterion
mutation or version criteria independently while also advancing the source aggregate revision.
Persist immutable source revisions containing criteria. Add concurrent update/delete tests.
