ID: P2-010
Priority: P2 Medium
Category: Frontend pagination / data accessibility
Status: Confirmed

Defect
The project dashboard and project user-story page call paged endpoints without page/size parameters,
then render only items. They ignore hasNext/totalPages and provide no pagination or load-more control.

Evidence
- frontend/src/pages/ProjectDashboardPage.tsx:38-43,105 onward
  requests /api/v1/projects and maps items only.
- frontend/src/pages/ProjectPage.tsx:113-119,218 onward
  requests /user-stories and maps items only.
- Backend controller defaults these collections to size 20.

Impact
Users with more than 20 projects or more than 20 stories in a project cannot reach later records
through the normal UI. The omission is silent and resembles data loss.

Remediation
Add server-driven pagination or an accessible incremental loader, display totals, retain filters/page
in the URL, and test boundary counts 20, 21, and multiple pages.
