ID: P2-007
Priority: P2 Medium
Category: Provider reliability / retry contract
Status: Confirmed exception-path gap

Defect
GenerationService performs one retry only after GenerationValidationException. The OpenAI adapter
maps malformed JSON, empty structured output, and incomplete responses to GenerationProviderException,
so malformed structured output never receives the documented controlled retry.

Evidence
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/generation/application/GenerationService.java:239-252
  catches only GenerationValidationException around the first call.
- backend/src/main/java/com/testforge/generation/provider/OpenAiTestGenerationProvider.java:82-114
  throws GenerationProviderException for incomplete, empty, refusal, or malformed output.
- SECURITY.md:27 and docs/THREAT_MODEL.md:17 describe one controlled retry for invalid output.

Impact
Recoverable malformed model responses fail the run immediately, reducing reliability and diverging
from the declared provider-validation contract.

Remediation
Distinguish retryable structured-output defects from nonretryable refusal, authentication, and
transport failures. Retry exactly once for the former under a total timeout/budget. Add adapter/service
tests for malformed JSON, empty output, refusal, 4xx, 5xx, timeout, and second-failure behavior.
