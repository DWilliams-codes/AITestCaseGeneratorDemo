ID: P3-002
Priority: P3 Low
Category: Frontend configuration / credential hygiene
Status: Confirmed

Defect
The production frontend bundle always prefills a public demo email and password, while the default
backend and Compose configuration disable demo seeding.

Evidence
- frontend/src/pages/LoginPage.tsx:43-48 hardcodes demo@testforge.local / TestForge!Demo2026.
- backend/src/main/resources/application.yml:96-97 sets seed-enabled false.
- docker-compose.yml:42 defaults TESTFORGE_DEMO_SEED_ENABLED to false.

Impact
Most deployments present credentials that cannot work, encourage accidental submission of a known
password, and make a mistakenly enabled demo account immediately discoverable.

Remediation
Leave production login fields empty. Inject demo defaults/copy only in an explicit demo build/profile
with a visible nonproduction banner and isolated synthetic data.
