ID: P3-003
Priority: P3 Low
Category: Frontend audit/history pagination
Status: Confirmed

Defect
The test-case history drawer requests only 20 revisions and 20 matching audit events, then renders
items without next/previous controls or a truncation notice.

Evidence
- frontend/src/pages/RequirementPage.tsx:1136-1149 sends size=20 for both requests.
- same file:1159-1232 maps items and never reads hasNext/totalPages.

Impact
Older review evidence silently disappears from the UI, which can mislead users investigating a long-
lived case even though the records still exist server-side.

Remediation
Add pagination/load-more controls with totals for both collections and preserve the selected revision
while loading additional pages. Test histories of 20 and 21 records.
