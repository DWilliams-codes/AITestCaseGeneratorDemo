ID: P2-012
Priority: P2 Medium
Category: Human review quality
Status: Confirmed presentation omission

Defect
The primary TestCasePanel exposes title, objective, priority/risk, mapped keys, preconditions, steps,
and final outcome, but not test data, test-data references, rationale, automation-candidate flag, or
coverage intent. Approval actions are available on that incomplete view.

Evidence
- frontend/src/pages/RequirementPage.tsx:940-1073 contains the panel and review buttons.
- The hidden fields are present in frontend/src/types/api.ts and editable later in the same page at
  approximately lines 1319-1594.

Impact
Reviewers can approve a case without seeing data sensitivity/strategy, broken step data references,
the provider rationale, or other structured evidence that affects safety and traceability.

Remediation
Render the complete normalized case in the review surface, including named data references and
sensitivity. Consider requiring the case accordion/evidence sections to be visited before approval.
Add a component test asserting every review-relevant field is visible before the approve action.
